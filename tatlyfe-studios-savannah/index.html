A husband-and-wife shop

Tatlyfe Studios has been tattooing in Savannah since 2011, run by a husband-and-wife team, not a rotating cast of chairs. There's no flash wall and no piercing counter here. Every piece is designed for the person wearing it, built from a real conversation, not pulled off a sheet.

The result is a shop that feels more like a private studio than a storefront: a handful of clients a week, real conversations before the needle ever comes out, and a space kept small on purpose.
