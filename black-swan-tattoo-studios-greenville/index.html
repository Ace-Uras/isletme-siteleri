01 / The studio

A gallery that happens to tattoo.

Faith Hudgens has been a working visual artist since 2013, showing bold, saturated paintings in galleries around the Upstate. She started tattooing in 2017, and in 2021 she opened Black Swan, becoming the first African American woman in South Carolina to run a licensed tattoo facility.

The through-line is craft. Every piece starts on paper, gets drawn to your body, and only then gets committed to skin. The team is women from a range of backgrounds, and the room is built to feel safe, unhurried, and genuinely yours.

“You will be judged for not pursuing your passions, and you will be judged for dreaming too big.”

Faith Hudgens, founder
02 / What we do

Four things, done properly.

  • Custom color & illustrative work

    Original designs built from a consult: painterly color, botanical detail, fine line. Nothing off a wall unless you want it.

  • Color on deep skin tones

    A specialty here, not an afterthought. Pigment choices and healing plans made for melanin-rich skin so the color stays true.

  • Cover-ups & reworks

    Old ink reimagined into something you’re proud of, assessed honestly in person before anything is booked.

  • Consultation first

    Every project opens with a sit-down: reference, placement, sizing, budget. You leave with a plan and a quote, no pressure.

03 / The collective
