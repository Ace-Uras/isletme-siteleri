The shop has been here since 2003

Wicked Ink opened on Clinton Highway in March 2003 and has been tattooing and piercing Knoxville ever since. The artists each carry over twelve years of experience, and the shop holds a 4.5 rating on Google.

It is a working shop, not a showroom. You can talk through an idea at the counter without booking anything first.
