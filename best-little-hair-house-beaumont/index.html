Run by Shelly, for the neighborhood.

Best Little Hair House In Texas has been a fixture on Eastex Freeway since October 1989. Shelly Haynes still runs the chair herself, and regulars keep coming back for the same reason they always have: a familiar face, a steady hand, and a shop that feels more like a friend's living room than a salon.

It's a real family shop. Kids get their first haircuts here, parents get their color touched up, and everyone leaves with the same easy, unhurried service.
