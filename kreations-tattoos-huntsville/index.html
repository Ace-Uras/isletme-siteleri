Since 1999.

Kreations Tattoos & Body Piercing opened its doors in Huntsville in 1999 and has been family-owned and operated ever since, making it, by most local accounts, the oldest tattoo studio still running in the city.

That kind of longevity isn't an accident. It's a laid-back, clean, family-friendly shop where the same faces have been welcoming clients year after year. The numbers back it up too: a 4.8 rating and an A+ accreditation from the Better Business Bureau. That's trust earned one appointment at a time, not bought with an ad campaign.
