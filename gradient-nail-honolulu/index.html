How it works

Manicure and pedicure,
at the same time.

01

Two technicians, one appointment

Regulars mention this first: instead of waiting through a manicure before a pedicure can start, two technicians work at once. Same visit, half the chair time.

02

Pedicures that don't rush the callus work

The luxury pedicure isn't a quick buff and polish. Callus care gets real attention, not a step skipped to move to the next chair.

03

Ombré, rhinestone, detail work

Beyond a standard set, the shop is known for ombré fades and rhinestone detailing: the kind of nail art that takes a steadier hand than a quick fill.

Behind the work

The names that come up in the reviews

Jenny, Lily, Yen, and Anna: mentioned again and again for careful shaping, even on fragile or damaged nails, and for taking the time a detail needs.
