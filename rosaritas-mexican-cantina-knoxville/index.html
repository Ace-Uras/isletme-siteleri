From the kitchen

A few of the plates from the menu. The full menu is at the counter, or on DoorDash and Uber Eats.

  • Steak fajitas

    The house specialty. Strips of steak with peppers and onions, brought out still sizzling.

  • Chile colorado

    Slow-cooked beef in a deep red chile sauce, served with rice, beans and tortillas.

  • Enchiladas Oaxaca

    Rolled enchiladas finished in the Oaxacan style, sauced and plated hot.

  • Chimichanga

    A crisp fried burrito, filled and topped the traditional way.

  • Carnitas

    Braised pork served with the usual company: rice, beans and fresh tortillas.

  • Street tacos

    Small doubled tortillas, cilantro and onion. Order them by the plate.

  • Birria quesatacos

    Griddled tacos filled with birria and melted cheese, with consomé for dipping.

  • Quesadilla fajita

    A loaded quesadilla built on the same sizzling fajita filling.

  • Shrimp cocktail

    Chilled shrimp in a bright tomato broth, Mexican style.

  • Seafood dinners

    Shrimp and seafood plates, served as full dinners.

  • Margaritas y sangría

    Poured at the bar for dine-in guests.

New life on Magnolia

Rosarita's opened in the spring of 2026 inside a building many Knoxvillians still remember as the old Long John Silver's at 2816 E Magnolia Avenue. It brought a full-service, sit-down Mexican kitchen back to this stretch of East Knoxville.

The neighborhood noticed. Within months of opening, the cantina was holding a 4.9 rating on Google.
